PAGE 1 - First day: know and pack

REPORT READY

  • Monday, September 21: arrive 0500 (5:00 a.m.). Normal roll call afterward: 0630 unless directed otherwise.
  • Back into designated recruit parking, starting nearest the tennis-court side. Stay at your vehicle with belongings until directed.
  • Late/absent: call 410-887-7380 at least 30 minutes before reporting; notify required recruit leadership.

PAPERWORK + SUPPLIES

  • Black ink: Form 78, every field; N/A where needed; sign/date.
  • Form A: both sides. Complete Vehicle Registration Sheet.
  • Paperclip Form A + Form 78 + Vehicle Registration Sheet.
  • County recruits: OHR reminder letter.
  • Valid driver's license + second legal ID.
  • Manual; 4-inch binder; dividers; paper.
  • Two matching black-ink pens; correction tape; lint roller.
  • Pocket notebook: keep it with you.

PT BAG

  • All-black running shoes; navy T-shirt, shorts, sweatpants and hoodie.
  • Shorts: 8-10-inch inseam. Last name: white, about 2-inch block letters on shirt/hoodie backs.
  • Black gloves; mouth guard; combination lock.
  • Clear, named water bottle: at least 32 oz.

FEMALE RECRUIT CLOTHING

  • White long-sleeved button-down + plain white undershirt.
  • Khaki Dickies 774 work pants; Dockers-style alternative if unavailable.
  • Plain black garrison belt; navy/black crew or calf socks; polished black boots.
  • White/gray sports bra before recruit uniform; gray/navy/black with uniform.
  • Hair clear of collar/eyebrows; headgear must fit. No claw clips.
  • No makeup/artificial nails; clear nail polish only. No smartwatch.
  • Permitted stud earrings: remove for PT/force training.

KNOW BEFORE ARRIVAL

  • Be truthful; report Honor Code violations immediately.
  • Follow directions; be on time; use respectful greetings.
  • Phone off/silent in locker, subject to staff instructions.
  • Outside: buddy system. Inside: single file.
  • Uniforms required by Week 2.

PAGE 2 - Demerits: early enforcement

The manual does not specify a Day 1 start. Bold/arrow entries start immediately after the Week 1 discipline class. Other listed demerits start in Week 3. Follow all rules from arrival.

AFTER WEEK 1 DISCIPLINE CLASS

  • Phone/other electronic device in possession.
  • Missing doctor's slip for illness/injury.
  • Lateness.
  • Negligently failing to follow instructions.
  • Not completing demerit run/assigned punishment.
  • Conduct unbecoming a Department member.
  • Smoking/vaping; tobacco possession at training site.
  • Traffic or civil-law violations.
  • Wearing uniform/PT clothes to stores/restaurants.
  • Social-media violation.
  • Unauthorized/excess computer or radio access/use.
  • County OIT security-policy violation.
  • Improper Training/Department records handling.
  • Leaving assignment/activity without permission.
  • Losing/negligently damaging police equipment.
  • Unauthorized person using Department equipment; unauthorized use of non-approved equipment.
  • Unauthorized secondary employment.
  • Absent Without Leave (AWOL).
  • Insubordination to staff/Department member.
  • Negligent safety-protocol violation.
  • Willfully disobeying a direct order.
  • Negligent misstatement/misrepresentation of fact.
  • Willful safety-protocol violation.
  • Fighting.
  • Honor System: lying, deception, cheating, plagiarism.
  • Disclosing confidential police information.
  • Refusing urinalysis testing.
  • Intoxication/unauthorized alcohol use on duty.
  • Criminal or serious traffic-law violations.
  • Zero-tolerance violation: racial, sexual, etc.

OTHER LISTED DEMERITS: WEEK 3

  • Five accumulated non-demerit infractions.
  • Missing required notification.
  • Not prepared for duty: uniform, equipment, etc.
  • Not checking email before duty.
  • Failure to appear in court.
  • Chain-of-command violation.
  • Missing assignment deadline.
  • Sleeping in class.
  • Fraternization.
  • Soliciting/accepting gratuities.
  • Not notifying supervisor of upcoming court case as defendant.

IF CORRECTED OR CITED

  • Rules still apply before demerit start dates. Non-demerit correction may be assigned.
  • Send directed violation report email before next roll call. Include incident and prevention plan.
  • Standard demerit run: 1.5 miles per infraction; separate incidents on separate dates.
  • Run with another recruit; both in PT uniform; scheduled training day, before/after training, by deadline.
  • Submit completion form + notification email as directed.

PAGE 3 - Conduct: learn first

HONOR SYSTEM

  • Tell the truth about duties, conduct, observations and Department business.
  • No false statements, cheating, plagiarism, unauthorized aids or assistance.
  • Do not share test questions/answers. Keep eyes on your own work.
  • Read, initial and date test integrity statement.
  • Report known Honor Code violations immediately to Training Staff.
  • Violations can result in dismissal.

CHAIN OF COMMAND

  • Squad leader → vice president → president → squad officer → squad sergeant → ELT lieutenant → Training Commander → Department chain.
  • Do not skip levels for routine issues.
  • Personal/confidential issues may go to assigned squad officer/sergeant.
  • Question apparent unlawful/policy-conflicting instructions respectfully with issuing authority.
  • Reporting policy/law violations: squad officer; if that officer is involved, squad sergeant.

PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

  • Department staff outside class: professional, work-related contact only, including texts/social media.
  • No police-facility visits except Department business/services.
  • Report conflicts such as Department relatives through chain; ELT Unit Commander approves exceptions.

GREETINGS + SALUTING

  • Use Sir/Ma'am/rank appropriately. Follow attention, classroom-entry and seating directions.
  • Required uniformed salute: initiate within six paces; face officer; eye contact; right hand empty.
  • Hold salute until returned/acknowledged; lower sharply.
  • Out-of-uniform staff and PT attire: appropriate greeting. No salute in bathrooms/locker rooms.
  • After "carry on," no repeated bracing/saluting of same instructor in same vicinity.
  • Uniformed Fire personnel: yield/greet; no brace/salute.

DAILY ROUTINE

  • Check message board: start, each break, lunch, before leaving.
  • Outside: partner at all times; more than two recruits in columns of two.
  • Between buildings: double-time unless instructed otherwise. Indoors: purposeful single file.
  • Secure empty classrooms/locker rooms; safeguard equipment.
  • No disruptive gatherings; clean up; complete duties before release.
  • No classroom food, candy, mints or gum without permission. Lunch: 30 minutes, cafeteria.
  • Beverages must not distract. No tobacco/nicotine use or possession at training sites; manual permits storage in vehicle.

PAGE 4 - Communication + academics

EMAIL: EVERY DAY

  • Check Outlook before duty/roll call; during breaks, lunch and study; before leaving.
  • No email during academic instruction.
  • Reply to Training Staff promptly, no later than end of day message was sent.
  • Each initial email/reply: greeting, paragraphs, professional closing, rank/name or full signature.
  • Greeting applies throughout email chains. Proofread spelling/grammar.

WRITTEN WORK + PENALTIES

  • Official correspondence to Training Staff: Form 12L. Use black ink for handwriting.
  • Clean, uncreased form; appropriate paragraphs; complete all corrections.
  • Formatting penalties start after first Unit L: Report Writing block.
  • 25 burpees per listed written violation: damaged/creased/stained form; missing paragraphs; 3+ spelling/grammar errors; second return for incomplete corrections.
  • 25 burpees per listed email violation: missing greeting, paragraphs, professional closing or rank/name/signature; 3+ spelling/grammar errors.

STUDY ROUTINE

  • Study/read outside class, including evenings/weekends.
  • Ask unit instructor for help early; use study groups and CCBC resources.
  • Class officers organize study groups with staff guidance.

SCORES TO MEMORIZE

  • Most units: 70% passing.
  • CEW/Taser: 100%.
  • NCIC: 80%.
  • First Aid: 80%.
  • CPR: 84%.
  • Passing grades alone do not guarantee continued employment.

PROBATION + RETESTS

  • Below 75% unit average: probation. Units with fewer than four tests: assessment begins at first quiz.
  • Document weekly improvement efforts.
  • Removal: 80% or better + at least two additional tests after probation begins.
  • Eligible retest: within two weeks of failure notification; remediation may precede.
  • Retest replaces final-exam grade; passing recalculated unit average is recorded as 70%.
  • No retest if mathematically unable to pass; three or more failed units means no retests.

ATTENDANCE

  • Attend required training; make up missed work regardless of reason.
  • More than 5% absence may prevent diploma eligibility.
  • After any sick leave: required doctor documentation + explanation.
  • On sick leave: remain home except medical treatment unless authorized.

PAGE 5 - Notifications + equipment

NOTIFY IMMEDIATELY, ON/OFF DUTY

  • Squad sergeant/officer: arrest; criminal charge or awareness of accusation; serious traffic violation.
  • Death in family; serious injury to recruit/family member; lost/stolen police equipment.

NOTIFY AT NEXT SHIFT

  • Official contact with sworn law enforcement; court involvement.
  • Circumstances affecting recruit position or reflecting negatively on recruit/Department.
  • Report address, phone, marital-status or long-term vehicle changes; update paperwork.

WEAPONS + ELECTRONICS

  • No off-duty firearm carry; Department weapons only as authorized for training. Store properly.
  • No knives/edged weapons at duty assignment, including utility-tool blades. If brought accidentally, secure in vehicle out of view.
  • Personal electronics generally prohibited; phone off/silent in locker as directed.
  • Special possession permission: ask squad supervisor; normally expires that day.
  • Authorized phone: silent/concealed; check at permitted times. Before testing, tell instructor about device/storage.
  • Department phones: official use; urgent personal calls need permission.

LAPTOP

  • Official Academy use only; complete Form 320 usage log during specified first-week period.
  • No shared passwords; report compromise immediately and change password.
  • Changes to hardware/software require approval.
  • Never leave laptop in vehicle. Lock in recruit classroom if not taking home.

RADIO + CAMERA

  • Protect issued radio; report loss/theft/damage immediately through squad leadership.
  • Wear after issuance/training; use only authorized channels/times/purposes.
  • End of day: radio off; used battery charging; charged unused battery on radio; store as directed.
  • No off-campus radio without explicit permission; authorized off-duty use only in bona fide emergency.
  • Accidental off-duty transmission: tell dispatcher; email squad sergeant/officers.
  • Body camera: directed training only; do not record staff/active-duty members without authorization.
  • Return all issued equipment when leaving program.

CLASS LEADERSHIP + RIDE-ALONGS

  • Follow lawful class-leadership directions; leaders allocate duties fairly and maintain accountability.
  • Class funds: separate from personal money; accurate records; safeguard funds.
  • Training Commander approves class activities/fundraising; Chief approves public Department name/logo use.
  • Ride-along: confirm precinct/time; arrive 20 minutes early; prescribed uniform + ballistic vest.
  • Bring weather gear, food requiring no heating and water.
  • Observe; follow officer directions. Recruits remain civilians: no weapons, arrests or participation in calls.
  • Use police equipment only as expressly instructed; manual has narrow extreme-emergency provision.
