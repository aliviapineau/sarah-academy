Rules & Regulations

Important guidelines for recruits.

First Day: Know and Pack

  • Report Ready: Arrive 0500 (5:00 a.m.). Normal roll call afterward: 0630 unless directed otherwise.
  • Late/Absent: Call 410-887-7380 at least 30 minutes before reporting.
  • Paperwork + Supplies: Black ink for forms. Pocket notebook: keep it with you. 4-inch binder, dividers, paper, correction tape, lint roller.
  • PT Bag: All-black running shoes; navy T-shirt, shorts, sweatpants, hoodie. Clear, named water bottle: at least 32 oz.
  • Female Recruit Clothing: White long-sleeved button-down + plain white undershirt. Khaki Dickies 774 work pants. Plain black garrison belt. No makeup/artificial nails; clear nail polish only. No smartwatch.

Conduct & Honor System

  • Honor System: Tell the truth about duties, conduct, observations and Department business. Report known Honor Code violations immediately to Training Staff.
  • Greetings + Saluting: Use Sir/Ma'am/rank appropriately. Required uniformed salute: initiate within six paces; face officer; eye contact; right hand empty.
  • Daily Routine: Outside: partner at all times; more than two recruits in columns of two. Indoors: purposeful single file. No classroom food, candy, mints or gum without permission.

Communication & Academics

  • Email: Check Outlook before duty/roll call; during breaks, lunch and study; before leaving. No email during academic instruction.
  • Scores to Memorize: Most units: 70% passing. CEW/Taser: 100%. NCIC: 80%. First Aid: 80%. CPR: 84%.
  • Probation: Below 75% unit average: probation. Removal: 80% or better + at least two additional tests after probation begins.

Demerits (Early Enforcement)

Starts immediately after Week 1 discipline class:

  • Phone/other electronic device in possession.
  • Missing doctor's slip for illness/injury.
  • Lateness.
  • Negligently failing to follow instructions.
  • Smoking/vaping; tobacco possession at training site.
  • Honor System violations: lying, deception, cheating, plagiarism.

Starts Week 3:

  • Not prepared for duty: uniform, equipment, etc.
  • Failure to appear in court.
  • Sleeping in class.
